### Reviewer conduct checklist (self-check before submitting)
- [ ] Judged the work, not the author; tone is professional and constructive.
- [ ] Every weakness names a consequence and, where possible, a fix.
- [ ] Scores are consistent with the written comments.
- [ ] No fabricated praise or criticism; claims trace to specific sections.
- [ ] Used the instructor's official ICSE rubric where it differs from this scale.
